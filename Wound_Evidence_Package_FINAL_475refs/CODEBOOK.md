S8 assigns a single dominant AI category per study. `Code_0_1 = 1` means feature supported in the curated extraction; `0` means not documented there, not proof of absence.

The source mapping retains article identity using title, embedded EndNote article metadata, and available DOI data. Missing DOIs were not guessed or synthesized. Changes in source membership and removed preprints are documented in `README.md`. Charts are descriptive within this non-systematic selection and should not be interpreted as calibrated risk metrics, model-quality rankings, population-level publication prevalence, or validated treatment efficacy.
